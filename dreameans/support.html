Dreameans Support

Record last night's dream, before it fades.

Dreameans is a dream journal built for the moment you wake up. Speak or type your dream in seconds, tag it with motifs, and look back over your nights in a dream calendar. Everything stays on your iPhone — private by design.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Dreameans has no accounts and no servers. Your dreams never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them. You can also export your dreams as a file from the App's settings at any time.

Can I share my dreams with friends?

Not in this version. Dreameans is currently a private journal: every dream is visible only to you, on your device. Sharing features are planned for a future version and will always be your choice, dream by dream.

How does voice input work?

Tap the record button and speak. The App uses Apple's speech recognition, with on-device recognition where available, and turns your voice into a dream entry. It needs microphone and speech-recognition permission; if you prefer, you can always type instead.

What are motifs?

Motifs are recurring dream themes — flying, being chased, water, falling, school, and more. The App suggests motifs from what you wrote, and you can add or remove them. The Motifs tab gathers every dream that shares a theme.

How do I delete a dream?

Open the dream from the feed or the calendar and use the delete button on its detail screen.

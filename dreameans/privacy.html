Privacy Policy — Dreameans

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Sato's creator ("we", "the developer") provides the iPhone app "Dreameans" (the "App"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Your dreams stay on your device

Everything you record in the App — your dream entries, keywords, motifs, reactions and comments — is stored only on your own device. The App has no account system and no servers, and none of this data is sent anywhere. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

2. No data collection

The App makes no network requests of its own. It contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and we do not build profiles of users.

3. Voice input

Voice input uses Apple's built-in speech recognition, with on-device recognition required where it is available. Your audio and your dream text are never sent to us — we operate no servers that could receive them.

4. Third parties

Your information is not provided to any third party.

5. Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device and we hold none of it, you can review, change or delete all of it at any time inside the App, export it from the App's settings, or delete it by deleting the App. If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features change. The current version is always available on this page, and changes take effect when posted here.

MonthSum Support

All your subscriptions. One monthly total.

MonthSum shows every subscription you have as a single monthly total in euros. Pick a service from the catalog, confirm the amount you actually pay, and MonthSum adds it up — no bank connection, no account, everything stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. MonthSum has no accounts and no servers of its own. Please note that deleting the app also deletes your registered subscriptions.

Why does the converted amount say "indicative"?

Amounts in other currencies are converted using European Central Bank reference rates (via the Frankfurter API). These are reference rates, so the converted figure is an indication — the amount your provider actually charges may differ slightly.

A price in the catalog is wrong. What should I do?

Catalog prices are listed prices and can change. On the confirmation screen you can always adjust the amount to what you actually pay. If you notice a wrong catalog price, let us know by email and we will update the catalog.

A service I use is not in the catalog.

You can register any subscription manually with the "Add manually" option. Feel free to also tell us which service is missing — the catalog is updated regularly.

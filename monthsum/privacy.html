Privacy Policy — MonthSum

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Sato's creator ("we", "the developer") provides the iPhone app "MonthSum" (the "App"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Your data stays on your device

The subscriptions you register in the App — names, prices, billing dates and related entries — are stored only on your own device. The App has no account system, and none of this data is sent to servers operated by the developer. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

2. Network requests the App makes

The App connects to the internet only for the following purposes:

  • Service catalog. The App downloads a catalog of well-known subscription services and their listed prices, so you can register a subscription without typing everything by hand. This download does not include any of your data.
  • Exchange rates. To show your monthly total in euros, the App retrieves reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API, which publishes European Central Bank reference rates. The App sends only currency codes — never your subscription data or personal information.

As with any internet request, the operators of those services may technically receive information such as your device's IP address. We do not control their handling of such technical information; please refer to their respective policies.

3. No advertising, analytics or tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and we do not build profiles of users.

4. Third parties

Except for the network requests described in section 2, your information is not provided to any third party.

5. Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device and we hold none of it, you can review, change or delete all of it at any time inside the App or by deleting the App. If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at the address below.

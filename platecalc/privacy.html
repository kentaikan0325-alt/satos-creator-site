Privacy Policy — PlateCalc

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sato's creator ("we", "the developer") provides the iPhone app "PlateCalc" (the "App"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Your data stays on your device

The settings you make in the App — your plate inventory, bar and collar preferences, warm-up percentages and saved favorites — are stored only on your own device. The App has no account system, and none of this data is sent anywhere. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

2. No network requests

The App works fully offline. It makes no network requests, contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and we do not build profiles of users.

3. Third parties

Your information is not provided to any third party.

4. Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device and we hold none of it, you can review, change or delete all of it at any time inside the App or by deleting the App. If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at the address below.

5. Contact

satoscreator@gmail.com

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features change. The current version is always available on this page, and changes take effect when posted here.

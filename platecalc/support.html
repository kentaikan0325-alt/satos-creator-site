PlateCalc Support

Enter a target weight. See exactly what to load.

PlateCalc is a barbell plate calculator. Type in the total weight you want, and PlateCalc shows which plates go on each side of the bar — as a to-scale diagram, with warm-up sets worked out for you. It works in kilograms and pounds, fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. PlateCalc has no accounts and no servers. Your settings and favorites never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them.

The total shown is not exactly my target. Why?

With a limited set of plates, some totals cannot be built exactly. In that case PlateCalc loads the closest total that your plates can make — the lighter one when two are equally close — and always shows the actual total it arrived at, so nothing is silently rounded away.

My gym doesn't have some of these plates.

Open Settings and adjust the plate inventory: switch plates off, set how many of each you have per side, or add your own plate sizes. There is a separate inventory for kilograms and pounds, and your changes are remembered.

Can I change the bar weight?

Yes. Choose a 20 kg / 45 lb bar, a 15 kg / 35 lb bar, a 10 kg bar, or enter a custom bar weight. You can also add your collar weight per side in Settings.

How do warm-up sets work?

The Warm-up tab builds a ladder from your target — empty bar, then 40%, 60%, 80% and 100% — and shows the plates for every step. The percentages can be edited in the Warm-up tab or in Settings.
